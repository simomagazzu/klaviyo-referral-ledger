# Klaviyo Referral Ledger

Track a Klaviyo partner's referral revenue share: what each quarter should pay, and whether referral windows and payouts are correct.

The sample file is fictional: `src/lib/ledger/__fixtures__/referral-export-sample.txt` (7 lines, total payout $113.00). It exists so the app and the tests can run without a real Partner Portal export.

## Scope

- Referral revenue share only. Managed accounts are out of scope.
- No insights, forecasts, or recommendations on screen. An open question is asked only when a check cannot be resolved from the export.

## Data input

Paste the "Referral Revenue Share Details" table from the Klaviyo Partner Portal into **Add data**.

Format as copied (one value per line, blank lines between header cells):

```
Partner Account Name | Customer Name | Revenue Share Start Date | Revenue Share End Date |
Payment Date | Product | Customer Payment Amount Received | Revenue Share Earnings % |
Split % | Partner Earnings
```

- `Split %` is often empty, so a record has 9 or 10 values. If the value after the rate is a percentage, it is the split.
- Dates look like `Aug 19, 2026`. Amounts look like `$1,234.56` and can be negative (refunds).
- Tab-separated rows (copied as a table) are accepted too.
- A Managed Accounts export (header contains `Managed Earnings %`) is rejected.
- Pasting is idempotent. The export is cumulative, so paste the full export every time. Identical lines can legitimately repeat, so the ledger keeps the highest count of each line seen in a single paste.

## Business rules

1. Payout per line = Revenue Share Earnings % × Customer Payment Amount Received.
2. Quarter = calendar quarter of the Payment Date (Q1 = Jan/Feb/Mar). The screen shows what each quarter should pay. Payout timing is separate.
3. The rate is fixed by the partner tier at lead acceptance and does not change for that referral.
4. Revenue Share Start Date is the referral start. The window lasts a fixed number of months from it.
5. Inside a window, every month with client spend must produce a referral line. A window stops early only if the client churned from that product. Otherwise a missing month or an early end is a check.
6. Months per rate: 15% → 16 months (stated by Klaviyo). 20% → 24 and 10% → 12 are inferred from typical uncut windows. Change them in `src/lib/ledger/config.ts`.

## One referral, several rows

Klaviyo sometimes splits one referral into two rows (the second often has `Split % = 100%`). Join rows of the same client, product, and rate whose dates touch or overlap. Do not join different rates.

The sample does this with `northwind.example` SMS at 15%:

| Client | Product | Rows in the sample | Joined window |
|---|---|---|---|
| northwind.example | SMS | Jul 31, 2025 → Aug 19, 2026 + Aug 19, 2026 → Nov 30, 2026 | Jul 31, 2025 → Nov 30, 2026 (16.0 months) |

`harbor.example` has a 15% Email window and a 20% Email window that overlap. They stay separate.

## What the sample is for

- `northwind.example` SMS: two exported rows, one referral.
- `harbor.example`: two rates, not joined. Each has a single payment, so **To check** asks about the gap.
- `cedar.example` SMS 20%: one payment in a 24-month window. The question is whether the client churned. `pnpm db:seed` records the answer "churned".
- `maple.example` SMS 15%: the exported end date runs a few days past 16 months. The check uses the 16-month cap, so the extra days are past the window and are not a missing month.

## Views

1. **Add data**: paste box, **Add to ledger**, a result message (added / skipped / unreadable lines), backup download and open, delete-all with an in-page confirmation.
2. **Clients**: one row per client, then its referrals. Active, expiring soon (ends within 60 days), or expired.
3. **Quarters**: year and Q1–Q4. One row per referral with payments in the quarter. A row is marked when the window length is not the expected months, or the actual payout % is not the rate.
4. **To check**: failed checks and open questions.
